How samples, preparations and analytical profiles relate
Voucher
The herbarium specimen that documents the plant. Every sample links to at least one.
Sample
Plant material collected for the study, such as dried aerial parts from one population.
Preparation
What was made from a sample: an essential oil, extract, fraction or isolate, with its method and yield. A preparation can be published on its own, without results.
Run (analytical profile)
One analysis of a preparation, such as a GC–MS run, with its compounds and amounts. Runs usually come from a results table.
Deposition
A citable, versioned publication of selected vouchers, preparations and runs.
