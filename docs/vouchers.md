# Vouchers

A voucher is the preserved specimen that results refer to. Curators register and release vouchers; researchers link their samples to them.

## Register a voucher

Choose **Curation → Vouchers → Register a voucher**. Enter what the label says: collector and number, collection date (or **Date as written on the label** for partial dates such as "May 1972"), the collection site, and the name as written. Under **Identification**, search for the taxon: a local match is used directly, a GBIF match is added first. Holding institution, collection and accession number identify the specimen; accession numbers are unique within a collection.

## Release to the public

A new voucher is private. On its page, **Release record** makes it public and asks how much of the locality to show:

- **Withheld**: no location at all.
- **Country and region**: country and region only.

Exact coordinates, locality, habitat, labels and collector names are never public. Photographs are approved one by one and appear only on released vouchers; the public copy is re-encoded without metadata such as GPS. Withdrawing a voucher hides it, its photographs and its linked chemistry again.

Publishing a deposition releases the vouchers it contains, so they do not have to be released by hand first (see [Depositions](depositions.md)).

## Redetermine or correct

- **Redetermine** records a new identification. The previous determinations stay in the voucher's history.
- **Edit collection details** corrects a mistake in the record. A reason is required; the change, its author and the old values are logged.

A published deposition keeps the determination it was published with. To update it, publish a correction of the deposition.

## Who sees what

| Role | Sees |
| --- | --- |
| Visitor | Released vouchers, approved photographs, published depositions. |
| Researcher | Also exact sites, labels and full-resolution photographs, and their own studies. |
| Curator | Everything; registers, edits and releases records and publishes depositions. |
